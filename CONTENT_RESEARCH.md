## Publication rule

`verified` means the item-level source, creator or rights holder, license, and intended use were checked. Only verified records may become playable audio, reproduced text, or displayed photography. `needs-review` means the material may be useful but one or more permissions, provenance, or metadata fields remain unresolved. `rejected` means it is unsuitable under the current archive policy. Free access alone is never treated as reuse permission.

## Adding the author's own photographs later

For each original photograph, retain the original file and add: an accurate alt description, caption, date, exact place, photographer name, consent/context note where people are identifiable, and a rights statement chosen by the photographer. Mark `sourceType` as `original-fieldwork`; do not label it Creative Commons unless the photographer deliberately selects that license. Keep imported archive images marked `external` so visitors can distinguish documentary fieldwork from third-party reference photography.
